The House That Reads

The house is built out of everything anyone ever wrote down and left behind. It has read all of it, it is quite alone, and it would very much like to narrate your visit. You are the guest. You do not have to do as you are told.

  • Click anything picked out in amber, or type what you do: open the door, look at the mat, sit.
  • Tab cycles what you could do to a thing, from the expected to the less expected. ↑ repeats. help says what's in the room.
  • You can also just ask it things. who are you is a fair start.
  • There are twenty-one ways to leave. Ctrl+C starts over; G after an ending shows every way out.
